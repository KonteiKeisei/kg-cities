KG Cities interface

Mockup of the interaction layer for every city: one look shared by the hover card, search, the GM's edit mode and the point-of-interest editor, matched to the map's own colours. Gold marks points of interest, cyan marks safe havens.

  • Players: hovering a landmark shows a card with its picture and story; Ctrl+F opens search for landmarks, streets, places and addresses, with distance from your view.
  • GM: edit mode shows a banner with what clicks do; clicking a plain building offers a quick menu; the editor docks to the right edge instead of a pop-up, so the map stays in view.
