# Data license

The city packs in `data/cities/` (landmark footprints, the 1980s eras: towers since built,
cleared sites, closed streets, period transit) and the transit lines in `data/transit/` are
derived from **OpenStreetMap** data, © OpenStreetMap contributors, and are made available under
the **Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0**: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

You are free to share, adapt and use this data, as long as you credit OpenStreetMap and keep
any adapted database you make public under the ODbL. OpenStreetMap's copyright and license:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The landmark descriptions in the same files were written for KG Cities and are offered under
the same terms as the data they sit in.

Map tiles are not shipped: each GM's Foundry downloads them from OpenFreeMap (or the tile
server set in the module settings) when a city is generated. They carry the same OpenStreetMap
license, and © OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles.
